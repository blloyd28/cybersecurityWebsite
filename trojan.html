The definition of a trojan horse virus is as follows. A malicous software that disguises itself as a legitamate safe program to trick users into downloading it. The name is a referance to the odyssey where to get into Troy the greeks build a horse and disguise it as a gift so they can get into the city and then at night they leave the horse and let in the main force taking the city in a night.

Phishing

What is it?

Phishing is commonly sent via email and is pretending to be someone your not in order to get the target to click on or download something with malicous intent. There are more phishing emails every day so its important to understand what to look for.

  • Weird links
  • Slight changes in company or peoples names
  • Offers that seem to good to be true
  • Nonsense emails or unknown phone numbers

Prevention

Try and keep your email to yourself. Avoid giving it out to websites you will only use once espeically if its an unfamilar site. Make sure that you block all spam emails. Delete junk and garbage mail. Get practice identifying spam emails so you can visually identify the emails when they appear inside your mailbox.

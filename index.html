Hackers

Red Hat Hackers

Red hat hackers are in interesting group of people. Instead of waiting for authority from law enforcement or legal personal red hat hackers seek to punish criminals. Even though they are technically breaking the law they are trying to help society. Think of them as vigalanties. They have agressive tactics and are in the legal grey area.

White Hat Hackers

White hats are helping people and are in the good side of the legal department. With permission they seek bugs or glitches so they can help those orginizations and make sure that another hacker dosent expose or take advantage of these. White hat hackers can test for vulnerabilites and suggest how to fix them or fix them themselves.

Black Hat Hacker

Black hat hackers are what most people picture when they hear hacker. It is someone with malicous intent and wants to break into a system. They violate laws and focus on trying to steal disrupt or commit extortions They can be motivated by the same things as any other criminals but some of them are greed personal issues political motivations or even a desire for chaos.
